Part One

Living with a Grateful Heart

Five teachings of Dr. Hak Ja Han Moon on gratitude — the heart behind every voice in this bank.

01

Everything looks beautiful

“When you live with a grateful heart, everything around you looks beautiful, makes you happy, and allows you to share that happiness with others.”

Gratitude changes how we see. It turns an ordinary table into a feast, and the happiness it brings is never meant to stay with us alone — it is meant to be shared.

02

Spiritual nutrition

Dr. Hak Ja Han described a heart of gratitude as spiritual nutrition and the foundation where the mind and body unite.

Just as the body is fed at the table, the spirit is fed by thanks. When gratitude comes first, mind and body move together as one.

03

Grateful for everything

She emphasized being grateful for everything — including health, life itself, and living according to providence.

  • Health
  • Life itself
  • Living according to providence
04

The best greeting for a new day

Expressing gratitude to God, True Parents, our spouse and our loved ones first thing in the morning is the best greeting for a new day.

Begin tomorrow morning with one sentence of thanks — then record it here so it can encourage someone else.

05

Gratitude expands our hearts to embrace the world with the unconditional love of a parent

“A life of gratitude is a life of true happiness. When we offer gratitude to Heaven in every circumstance—both in joy and in trial—our hearts expand, and we can embrace the world with the unconditional love of a parent.”
